🎙️ Voice & Video Gatherings

Meet people through conversation. Cameras are optional, and you can mute your microphone whenever you like.

Start a gathering

Generate a unique invitation link, then share it privately with people you want to invite.

Join a gathering

The gathering opens in a separate browser tab. Your browser will ask permission before using your microphone or camera. Choose audio-only or leave your camera off if you prefer.

Important privacy and availability notes

This initial version uses the independent Jitsi Meet service, not SHONnetwork's own video servers. Its availability, moderator requirements, recording options, and privacy practices are controlled by that provider. A meeting link is not a guarantee of access control: anyone with the link may be able to enter. Do not share confidential information.

SHONnetwork account invitations, friendship checks, meeting moderation, and in-room games are not yet integrated into this page. We'll only describe those as working after testing them.

If the service asks for a moderator or account, follow its on-screen instructions. Never enter your SHONnetwork password on the video provider's site.
