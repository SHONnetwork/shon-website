🌍 A Network Built Around People

SHONnetwork is a people-centered network for discovering, forming, and sustaining compatible homes, households, extended families, and communities through voluntary, mutually beneficial relationships.

People may have rooms, homes, time, skills, knowledge, companionship, creativity, land, technology, or resources to contribute. Our goal is to make these possibilities easier to discover and develop together.

Participation in SHONnetwork should remain free. Partnerships may expand available services without requiring people to purchase anything merely to belong.

Our Proposed Partnership Model

A gaming company could develop, integrate, distribute, maintain, and operate participating games or game catalogs, including any appropriate payment systems.

SHONnetwork could introduce interested participants to those games and receive an agreed share of qualifying revenue where appropriate.

Free games must remain available to people who cannot afford paid titles. Optional paid games may coexist with free participation. No purchase should be required simply to participate in SHONnetwork.

No gaming-company partnerships or licensed games are represented as active unless separately confirmed.

💻 Technology, AI & Resource Collaboration

We envision a network that can help people discover relevant opportunities without requiring them to navigate countless disconnected systems.

Potential collaborators may help with:

Search & Discovery

Improve searches across people, homes, interests, communities, and compatible opportunities.

Resource Information

Connect reliable national, state, and local assistance directories with clear source attribution and updates.

Social Tools

Develop accessible messaging, moderated community chat rooms, multiplayer games, and other ways to connect.

Information-sharing partnerships would require appropriate permissions, privacy protections, technical agreements, and reliable update procedures.

🏘️ More Housing Through Existing Resources

Many places already exist but are vacant, underused, or poorly connected to people who could benefit from them.

SHONnetwork seeks partnerships that could help make these opportunities visible, including shared homes, caretaker-presence arrangements, cooperative households, and community initiatives.

Participants should be able to explore arrangements voluntarily, with clear expectations and mutual agreement.
