Why connections matter

Abundant resources. Too many people without a home. SHONnetwork helps people find one another, discover existing opportunities, and build mutually beneficial relationships.

SHONnetwork begins with a practical idea: the home is the opportunity, and the people are the heart of it. A room, a friendship, shared knowledge, a welcoming household, or a new community can begin with one connection.

The dollar figures are mathematical comparisons, not proposed spending programs. HUD count: January 2025 point-in-time estimate. They do not imply that everyone experiencing homelessness has the same housing needs.

A people-centered network

SHONnetwork exists to help people discover, form, and sustain compatible homes, households, extended families, and communities through voluntary, mutually beneficial relationships. It is not simply a rental directory or a dating site.

Some people have space to share. Others have skills, presence, friendship, experience, or ideas. SHONnetwork makes room for those possibilities without treating a person's income as the measure of their worth.

SHONnetwork is developing additional community, learning, social, and collaboration features. Features not yet available are not presented as active services.
