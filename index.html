🧭 Your SHON connection journey

These steps help you use SHON while matching and safety verification are being developed. Completing a step does not mean a household has been screened or approved.

  1. Create or update your member profile and decide what to share publicly.
  2. Save your private household requirements. Only you can edit these answers.
  3. Choose matching preferences and browse potential connections.
  4. Review mutual interest. This means both people selected Interested, not that requirements have been verified.
  5. Use private messaging to discuss arrangements. Do not send sensitive documents or payments to strangers.

Important: SHON has not yet verified compatibility, identity, background information, accessibility, affordability, or child-safety eligibility. Discuss essential terms independently before making commitments.

SHON Resource Intelligence · development roadmap

One question. Many trusted sources. Better-fitting answers.

SHON is preparing a resource-discovery system that can combine permissioned community-service directories, public agency information, and participating organizations' own updates. The goal is to help people find services that fit their actual circumstances, not just their ZIP code.

1. Search by real needs

Housing type, no-income eligibility, daytime access, disability accommodations, food, transport, hours, and preferred contact methods.

2. Trace every result

Show the original provider, source link, last update, eligibility notes, and whether availability is verified or unknown.

3. Keep providers involved

Invite authorized data partners and local organizations to supply or correct public service listings.

How resource searches work today: SHON helps you discover services using public directories and map searches. These searches open external websites in a new tab, and their listings, hours, eligibility, costs and availability are not verified by SHON. AI-assisted matching and a combined national SHON resource directory are still in development. SHON will seek appropriate data permissions and integration partners before importing directories. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public search queries.

Two independent networks. Different purposes. Shared possibilities.

Partnerships

SHON & SHANE: A partnership in development.

SHONnetwork and SHANEnetwork have distinct missions. SHON is being developed as a free, nonprofit-oriented housing opportunity network. SHANE is a separate commercial collaboration and opportunity network, also in development.

SHONnetwork

Supported Housing Opportunity Network

Housing possibilities, companionship, community building, and resource discovery. The planned network is free to members and focused on people rather than commercial rental transactions.

In development

SHANEnetwork

Shared Human Ambitions, Networks & Enterprises

A separate, planned commercial network for opportunities, partnerships, events, employment, enterprise, and collaboration.

Proposed website: shanenetwork.org (availability and launch not yet verified)

How the networks intend to work together

SHON may introduce people to relevant opportunities through appropriate, optional links. SHANE intends to support SHON through awareness and future revenue contributions. SHON's charitable mission and decisions will remain independent of SHANE's commercial interests.

SHANE's initial goal is to contribute 20% of qualifying gross revenue to SHON, subject to future circumstances and appropriate organizational arrangements. This is a prospective goal, not a claim that contributions are currently being made.

Different missions. Shared possibilities. Neither network is yet represented here as a registered tax-exempt charity or an operating commercial platform.

About SHON

Not about profit. About people.

SHON, the Supported Housing Opportunity Network, is a developing, free, nationwide, people-centered initiative. We hope to connect individuals, families, households, and communities around shared living, companionship, resources, and mutually beneficial opportunities. We are not a conventional rental marketplace.

Development notice: SHON is an early-stage network with account and listing features in testing. Legal protections, moderation, verification, and confidential reporting processes must be established before public matching features launch.
