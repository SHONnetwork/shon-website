# SHON release-candidate review with Alice

Status: Planned. Begin after the core SHON member journey works and passes release checks. Alice's findings have not yet been collected.

## Purpose
Ask Alice to evaluate SHON's actual usefulness for people forming voluntary shared homes, households, extended families, companionship, and communities, including people facing homelessness. SHON is not a rental marketplace.

## Review together
1. Registration and accessibility: Can someone without a permanent address, income, or reliable phone still participate?
2. Profile questions: Which are confusing, intrusive, unnecessary, missing, or liable to exclude people unfairly?
3. Household compatibility: Are accessibility needs, household boundaries, pets, family arrangements, contributions, location, and consent handled well?
4. Discovery and communication: Can people find compatible individuals and make safe voluntary connections without disclosing private information prematurely?
5. Real-world pathways: How well does SHON complement homeless programs, outreach organizations, shared-living opportunities, and community partners?
6. Resources and referrals: What information, categories, program partnerships, or verification processes are needed?
7. Safety and moderation: What reporting, blocking, safeguarding, and misinformation controls need improvement?
8. Language and inclusion: Does SHON respect people who need a home and people who wish to share or build one?

## Process
- Demonstrate the tested release candidate and existing questionnaire to Alice.
- Record her exact suggestions separately from our interpretations.
- Triage each item as release blocker, important follow-up, or optional enhancement.
- Make changes on a development branch, preserving existing data and privacy boundaries.
- Retest affected workflows before releasing.

Do not describe Alice as having approved SHON or endorsed any feature until she has actually reviewed it.
