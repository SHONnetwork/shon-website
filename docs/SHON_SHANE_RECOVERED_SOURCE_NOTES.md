# SHON and SHANE: recovered source archive (27 October 2026)

This record preserves concepts recovered from historical documents and images. It is NOT an instruction to replace working code or to treat proposals as operational facts.

## SHON's mission
Supported Housing Opportunity Network (SHON) is a free, people-centered national network for finding compatible people to form and sustain shared homes, households, extended families, companionship, and communities through voluntary relationships. SHON is not a rental marketplace. No conventional rental advertisements, property sales, paid access, or paid rankings. Four original entry points: I need a place; I have space to share; I can offer help; I'm looking for someone. Everyone may need and offer something simultaneously.

## SHANE's separate mission
Shared Human Ambitions, Networks & Enterprises (SHANE) is a separate proposed commercial network for collaborations, enterprise, partnerships, jobs, events and opportunities. Optional relevant referrals may connect the two networks. SHANE's proposed contribution of 20% of qualifying gross revenue to SHON is a prospective, adjustable goal, not a current payment or binding commitment. Preserve independent governance and transparent reporting.

## Historical visual identity
SHON: green foliage, home, human figures, rising sun, people and belonging. SHANE: blue and gold, rising city, human figure, forward movement and opportunities. Joint imagery depicts independent networks and shared possibilities. Preserve original high-resolution art files separately; this text record does not embed them.

## Institutional collaboration proposals
One free national public platform plus independently operated regional programs, co-designed with universities, nonprofits, legal and social-work programs, community organizations and technical partners. Potential workstreams: accessibility, governance, technology, legal templates, education, research, participant safeguards, grants and fiscal sponsorship. Partners may contribute limited capabilities. None is assumed to have agreed.

A proposed founding director fellowship or institution-sponsored remote project leadership role appears in the historical prospectus; it is a proposal, not an existing appointment.

SHON was described in the October 2026 outreach documents as not yet incorporated or IRS-recognized as tax-exempt. Do not present proposed legal status as achieved.

## Participation and trust
Email-only participation should be possible without mandatory phone, permanent address or income verification merely to register. Privacy controls, consent, blocking, reporting, accessible interfaces, and private communications matter. No public exact home addresses or private medical details. Do not promise staffed support, case management, emergency response or verified placements without actual capacity. Distinguish confirmed opportunities from proposals and information links. Housing-linked work and volunteer opportunities require genuine accommodation; general employment advertisements belong to SHANE, not SHON.
