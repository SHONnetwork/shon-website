## SHON's independence is non-negotiable
SHON v1 is a self-contained people/places/possibilities network focused on housing stability. Preserve its original accounts, Supabase project, profile privacy, optional answers, listings, Discover, search, messages, connections, community, resources, games and moderation. No SHANE prerequisite, database merge, shared master identity, automatic record transfer, unapproved mass messaging or production release. Existing SHON release audit remains authoritative for outstanding two-member verification.

## Where SHON can distinguish itself
The major roommate marketplace Roomies already offers free room/room-wanted listings, team-ups and private messaging (https://www.roomies.com/about). Independent nonprofit homeshare programs and the National Shared Housing Resource Center offer trained screening, agreements and follow-up (https://www.nationalsharedhousing.org/). HomeShare Online offers a nonprofit matching experience, agreements and partner support (https://info.homeshareonline.org/americorps).
SHON should complement these patterns with practical pathways for nonstandard housing stability: low/zero-cost arrangements, housemate compatibility, mutually agreed work/room-and-board, caretaking, relocation, hosts with unused rooms, partner-assisted accommodation and support relationships. These are product *opportunities*, not claims of existing inventory, approved partners or verified safeguards. Do not promise free housing, safety, medical care or guaranteed matches.

## SHON-first release and growth sequence
1. Keep v1 healthy: twice-daily-ish Supabase read-only original-project checks retained in the Coordination Desk, without artificial traffic or claims that activity prevents automatic pausing.
2. Demonstrate `docs/SHON_TWO_MEMBER_RELEASE_TEST.md` with two real authorized test accounts: profile save/reload and optional privacy, public vs private listings, Discover, introductions, blocked/revoked messaging, report functionality, keyboard navigation and mobile. Report real gates, not optimistic percentages.
3. Improve first 60 seconds: explain “people, places, possibilities” through an honest, welcoming browse/discover path, with clear options for people seeking accommodation, people with space, and local partners. Avoid mandatory long questionnaires and mandatory phone verification. Real accessible keyboard workflows precede decoration.
4. Establish legitimate pilot supply: owner-consented listing/verification process and partner outreach plan focused on one geographic area, then replicate. No fabricated profiles, speculative outreach or unverified claims of agency relationships.
5. Use original artwork and warm presentation; retain the independent SHON menu and identity. Useful additions belong in versioned draft PRs tested for user regressions.

## Consent-based SHON ↔ SHANE connection
SHANE is an endeavor-centered cooperation engine, **not SHON's parent platform**. Start with an optional versioned adapter that shares only a consented public description and purpose, source ownership URL, minimal approved scopes, expiry and revocation. Keep housing profiles/health/financial/background details entirely within SHON by default. No automatic shared login, contacts, housing detail copy or mixed permissions. Pilot with two authorized accounts and verify opt-in → discovery → revoke and privacy exclusion, including when either site is down.
Only after SHANE's complete endeavor journey and SHON's two-member journey are independently verified should a real adapter be considered for production.
