## SHON's independent development non-negotiables

- Preserve the first released version, existing opt-in Discover, members, profile editing, listings, connections, messaging, community tools, games, resources, accessible page paths and separate private story/optional questionnaire fields.
- Retain four entry invitations: "I need a place", "I have space to share", "I can offer help", "I'm looking for someone." A participant may need and offer at the same time.
- Do not make SHON a paid rental/listings site, property marketplace, paid-placement service or mandatory cellphone/financial-verification workflow. Do not equate a good match with verified housing or personal safety.
- Private story, support/medical details, unshared questionnaire answers and precise addresses must not be exposed through public discovery, SEO, APIs or SHANE integration. Follow appropriate consent, permissions, blocking and reporting. No promise of staffed human screening, caregiving, legal representation, emergency support or guaranteed placement absent actual provision.
- A controlled optional integration could let SHON participants opt into specific SHANE capabilities. It must have a stated purpose, minimal data disclosure, authorization and revocation. It must never require SHANE login to use SHON. Commercial support proposals are not actual payments.

## The SHON v1 test that comes before new expansion

Use [SHON's eight-step two-member test](SHON_TWO_MEMBER_RELEASE_TEST.md) with **two dedicated synthetic accounts**, not actual member data. Verify Discover public/private field separation; interest visibility opt-out; connection request/accept; database-enforced no-message-before-accept; private conversation and persistence; blocked/revoked-message denial; sign-out/return; keyboard and narrow-phone usability.

Do not call the full journey passed until each step has tested evidence (branch/commit, environment, time, actual browser/database assertion and reproducible results). The earlier separate draft static integrity gate covers **19 HTML pages and 366 local references**; it is valuable but does not prove member privacy or messaging. Preserve existing release instead of merging cosmetic changes.

## The highest-value SHON product decisions

1. **Credible first encounter:** in one short welcome experience, clarify SHON's no-fee, voluntary purpose and the four entry points; explain private vs publicly shared information before profile completion.
2. **Useful without a match:** show relevant verified community/resource information, allow saving search interests without public exposure, and make no invented listings or promises of access.
3. **Safety by design:** clear written terms and expectations, neutral due diligence for everyone, participant-controlled communication, leave/block/report and accessible explanations. Never profile people as trustworthy solely from algorithmic compatibility.
4. **Local proof before national scale:** recruit only genuine voluntary community partners with permission and operational capacity; start with a small realistic pilot. Do not advertise institutional arrangements that are still proposals.
5. **Accessibility as core infrastructure:** keyboard-first controls, readable mobile UI, email-only onboarding path without mandatory phone, optional answers, low-bandwidth behavior and verified recovery after interruption.
6. **Independence:** if SHANE introduces a new plugin, SHON should choose whether to integrate a stable optional adapter, never copy live housing data into SHANE by default.
