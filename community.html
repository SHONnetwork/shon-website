💬 Social & Games

You shouldn't need a housing listing to belong. SHONnetwork welcomes people who want to meet others, share interests, form friendships, and build compatible households or communities.

Private messaging and friendship requests are available through the member connections interface, subject to the site's current database configuration. Community chat rooms are available now. Multiplayer games within SHONnetwork are a future goal.

💚 More Than a Roof. A Life Shared on Your Own Terms.

SHONnetwork is a people-centered network for discovering, forming, and sustaining compatible homes, households, extended families, and communities through voluntary, mutually beneficial relationships.
